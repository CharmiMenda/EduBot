Act as a Senior AI Architect, NLP Engineer, and Full Stack Developer.

Design and implement a RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) based AI Assistant for a Placement Preparation Platform.

The AI assistant must help students prepare for placements by retrieving knowledge from:

company placement data

1️⃣ What is RAG (Explain in System)

The system must implement Retrieval Augmented Generation.

Workflow:

User Question
     ↓
Embedding Generation
     ↓
Vector Database Search
     ↓
Retrieve Relevant Documents
     ↓
LLM Generates Context-Aware Answer
     ↓
Return Personalized Response

This allows the AI assistant to answer questions using real stored knowledge instead of hallucinating.
